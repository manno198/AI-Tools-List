# YouMind

**Link:** https://youmind.com/
**Category:** AI Creative Tools

## What it is

YouMind is an AI studio for collecting and understanding source material, then turning it into finished creative work. Users can save webpages, videos, podcasts, and files, explore them with AI, and create writing, slides, images, videos, and webpages.

## Who it's for

Students, researchers, creators, educators, and professionals who want one workspace for learning from source material and developing it into shareable content.

## Key Features

- Save and organize webpages, videos, podcasts, notes, and files
- Ask questions and connect ideas across saved materials
- Generate articles, slides, images, videos, and webpages
- Use reusable community and personal skills
- Access through web, browser extension, desktop, and mobile apps

## Access

Web application at [youmind.com](https://youmind.com/), with companion apps and a browser extension.
