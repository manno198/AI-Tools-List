# Krea

**Link:** https://www.krea.ai/
**Category:** AI Creative Tools

## What it is

Krea is an AI creative suite for generating, editing, and enhancing images, videos, and 3D assets. It brings multiple models and real-time tools into one workspace.

## Who it's for

Designers, artists, agencies, and creative teams that want to explore visual ideas, produce assets, or refine existing media.

## Key Features

- Generate images and video from text and visual inputs
- Real-time image generation and editing
- Upscale and enhance images and video
- Train custom LoRA models for styles, products, or characters
- Generate 3D objects and connect model steps in visual workflows

## Access

Web application at [krea.ai](https://www.krea.ai/).
