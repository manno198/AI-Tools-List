# OpenArt

**Link:** https://openart.ai/
**Category:** AI Creative Tools

## What it is

OpenArt is an AI media creation workspace for generating and editing images, video, and audio. Its tools also support story production, with reusable characters and workflows that connect assets into sequences.

## Who it's for

Illustrators, video creators, marketers, and storytellers producing visual concepts, social content, ads, or short films.

## Key Features

- Generate and edit images from prompts and references
- Create videos from text or images using multiple models
- Maintain character consistency across generations
- Build storyboards and refine video sequences through chat
- Create audio and combine generated media in one workspace

## Access

Web application at [openart.ai](https://openart.ai/).
