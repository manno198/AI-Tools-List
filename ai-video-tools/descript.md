# Descript

**Link:** https://www.descript.com/
**Category:** AI Video Tools

## What it is

Descript is an AI powered video and audio editor built around text based editing. It transcribes recordings so creators can edit the media by changing the transcript, then refine and publish in the same workspace.

## Who it's for

Video creators, podcasters, marketers, educators, and teams producing tutorials, social clips, or internal communications.

## Key Features

- Edit video and audio by editing the transcript
- Record screens and remote conversations
- Remove filler words, clean audio, and adjust eye contact with AI
- Generate captions, translations, clips, and AI video drafts
- Collaborate on projects and share review links

## Access

Web and desktop applications with a free plan and paid tiers.
