# Luma AI

**Link:** https://lumalabs.ai/
**Category:** AI Video Tools

## What it is

Luma AI offers Dream Machine, a generative workspace for making and editing images and videos from text prompts and visual references. It is designed for rapid creative exploration, including remixing and iterating on ideas.

## Who it's for

Artists, designers, filmmakers, and creators developing visual concepts, image assets, and short video content.

## Key Features

- Generate images and videos from prompts
- Animate images and use visual references
- Remix and iterate on generations
- Creative controls for styles, characters, and camera movement

## Access

Dream Machine is available on the web and iOS.
