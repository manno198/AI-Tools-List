# Runway

**Link:** https://runway.com/
**Category:** AI Video Tools

## What it is

Runway is a generative AI platform for creating and editing video and other media. Users can start with text, an image, or existing footage, then refine, extend, upscale, and export their work.

## Who it's for

Filmmakers, video editors, designers, studios, and marketing teams creating or modifying visual content.

## Key Features

- Generate video from text, images, or video clips
- Edit footage with AI tools for removing objects, changing backdrops, and relighting scenes
- Extend and upscale generated clips
- Access multiple generative video models in one workspace
- Generate dialogue, voiceover, or music with supported tools

## Access

Web application at [runway.com](https://runway.com/).
