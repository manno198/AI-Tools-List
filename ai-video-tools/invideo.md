# InVideo

**Link:** https://invideo.io/
**Category:** AI Video Tools

## What it is

InVideo is an AI video creation and editing platform that turns prompts or scripts into assembled videos. Its workflow can generate scenes, visuals, voiceovers, music, and edits, then lets users refine and export the result.

## Who it's for

Creators, marketers, and businesses making social media videos, ads, explainers, and other scripted content.

## Key Features

- Generate video from a text idea or script
- Assemble scenes, visuals, narration, music, and transitions
- Choose from multiple AI video models
- Review and refine the generated timeline
- Collaborate in real time

## Access

Web application at [invideo.io](https://invideo.io/).
