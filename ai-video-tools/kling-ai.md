# Kling AI

**Link:** https://kling.ai/
**Category:** AI Video Tools

## What it is

Kling AI is a generative media workspace for creating and editing videos and images. Its video tools accept text prompts and reference images, and support motion direction, character consistency, and generated audio features.

## Who it's for

Filmmakers, social creators, advertisers, and visual storytellers prototyping or producing AI assisted video and image content.

## Key Features

- Generate video from text prompts or images
- Create and edit AI images
- Direct motion and use reference elements
- Tools for character consistency, lip sync, and audio generation
- Additional creative tools such as avatars and motion control

## Access

Web application at [kling.ai](https://kling.ai/).
