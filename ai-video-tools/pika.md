# Pika

**Link:** https://pika.art/
**Category:** AI Video Tools

## What it is

Pika is an AI creative platform with tools for generating and editing video, images, and audio. Its video studio supports short clips and multi shot sequences, alongside focused apps for tasks such as motion transfer and extending footage.

## Who it's for

Social media creators, artists, and small creative teams making stylized clips, product visuals, and other AI media.

## Key Features

- Create videos and images with generative models
- Extend footage, transfer motion, and create product ads
- Dedicated tools for color grading and other video tasks
- Generate audio, speech, music, and sound effects

## Access

Web application at [pika.art](https://pika.art/).
