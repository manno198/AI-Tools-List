# PopAi

**Link:** https://www.popai.pro/
**Category:** AI Presentation Tools

## What it is

PopAi is an AI presentation maker and productivity workspace. Its presentation tools create slide deck drafts from a prompt or source material and help users refine the resulting content.

## Who it's for

Students, educators, professionals, and small teams preparing presentations from a topic, brief, or reference documents.

## Key Features

- Generate presentations from prompts and source documents
- Create structured slide drafts for different presentation goals
- Edit and refine generated slides
- Use AI tools for related document and content workflows
- Export presentations for continued use and sharing
