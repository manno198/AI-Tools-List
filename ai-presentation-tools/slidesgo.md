# Slidesgo

**Link:** https://slidesgo.com/
**Category:** AI Presentation Tools

## What it is

Slidesgo is a presentation template library that also offers an AI Presentation Maker. The maker generates a themed slide deck from a topic and lets users download it to continue editing in presentation software.

## Who it's for

Students, teachers, freelancers, and professionals who want a quick presentation draft with an established visual style.

## Key Features

- Generate presentations from a text prompt
- Choose from presentation styles and templates
- Download editable PPTX files compatible with PowerPoint and Google Slides
- Access a library of presentation templates and related AI tools
- Free usage is limited; account and plan limits may apply

## Access

AI Presentation Maker at [slidesgo.com/ai/presentation-maker](https://slidesgo.com/ai/presentation-maker).
