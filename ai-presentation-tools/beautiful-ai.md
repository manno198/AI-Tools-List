# Beautiful.ai

**Link:** https://www.beautiful.ai/
**Category:** AI Presentation Tools

## What it is

Beautiful.ai is presentation software that combines AI assisted deck creation with Smart Slides. Users can start from an idea or source material, refine a slide outline, and generate a designed presentation whose layouts adjust as content changes.

## Who it's for

Business teams, consultants, educators, and anyone who needs polished, consistent presentations without manually laying out every slide.

## Key Features

- Generate a slide by slide outline from a prompt or source documents
- Refine the presentation structure before designing slides
- Apply themes and consistent image styles
- Smart Slide layouts automatically realign and resize content
- Collaborate, present, and export to formats including PDF and PowerPoint

## Access

Web application at [beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai/).
