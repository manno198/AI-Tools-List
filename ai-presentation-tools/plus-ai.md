# Plus AI

**Link:** https://plusai.com/
**Category:** AI Presentation Tools

## What it is

Plus AI is a presentation assistant that works inside Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint. It can create presentations from prompts or documents and help users rewrite, add, remix, and reformat slides.

## Who it's for

Professionals and teams that want AI slide creation and editing within their existing presentation software and templates.

## Key Features

- Generate a presentation from a prompt, text, or source document
- Rewrite or add individual slides
- Remix and reformat existing slides
- Use AI generated images, icons, charts, and tables
- Apply custom templates and shared brand styles

## Access

Available as an add-on for Google Slides and PowerPoint through [plusai.com](https://plusai.com/).
