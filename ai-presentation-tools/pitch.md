# Pitch

**Link:** https://pitch.com/
**Category:** AI Presentation Tools

## What it is

Pitch is a presentation workspace for creating and delivering slide decks, with AI assistance and tools for team collaboration. It combines a slide editor, reusable brand styles, templates, and shared presentation workflows.

## Who it's for

Startups, sales teams, marketers, and other teams that create, review, and share presentations together.

## Key Features

- Create and edit presentations with AI assistance
- Collaborate live with comments and slide assignments
- Use reusable styles, templates, custom fonts, and animations
- Embed video and interactive content
- Share decks and track engagement

## Access

Web application at [pitch.com](https://pitch.com/).
