# Tome AI

**Link:** https://tomeapp.ai/
**Category:** AI Presentation Tools

## What it is

Tome AI at tomeapp.ai is an AI presentation service for creating and enhancing PowerPoint decks from prompts and source materials. The site also lists conversions such as PDF, Word, Excel, Markdown, and YouTube content to presentations.

This is the service at the supplied tomeapp.ai domain. It is distinct from Tome Slides at tome.app, whose presentation product was shut down in 2025.

## Who it's for

People who need to draft, restyle, or convert source documents and media into editable presentations.

## Key Features

- Generate presentations from a prompt or source document
- Beautify and enhance existing PPT files
- Convert formats such as PDF, Word, Excel, and Markdown into slides
- Create speaker notes and presentation videos
- Export editable PPTX files

## Access

Web application at [tomeapp.ai](https://tomeapp.ai/).
