# Vibiz.ai

**Link:** https://www.vibiz.ai/
**Category:** Autonomous Agents → Marketing / Business Operations

## What it is

Vibiz.ai bills itself as the first "Vibe Business Platform" — an AI-native platform that runs an entire marketing operation autonomously. Unlike traditional marketing tools that assist a human in completing tasks, Vibiz acts as an AI agent that takes ownership of the full marketing funnel: strategy, landing page creation, ad generation, and campaign management.

## Who it's for

Solopreneurs, startups, and small businesses that want the output of a professional marketing team without the cost or complexity of hiring one.

## Key Features

- AI-powered funnel building, ad creative generation, and landing page automation in one platform
- Extends the "vibe coding" concept to the business layer — the founder sets direction, the AI executes marketing, funnels, and growth strategy
- Automates workflows across Slack, WhatsApp, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Sheets, and thousands of other tools (lead enrichment, daily reports, etc.) with no code or flowcharts
- Works for any business relying on digital marketing: e-commerce, SaaS, service businesses, personal brands
